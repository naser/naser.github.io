### People

THREADS Lab is led by **Naser Ezzati-Jivan**, Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Brock University and principal investigator of the lab.

![Naser Ezzati-Jivan, Principal Investigator]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/naser-2026.jpg){: width="100%" :}

The lab brings together PhD and MSc students, undergraduate researchers, research assistants, and visiting interns working across software observability, performance engineering, trustworthy and agentic AI, and heterogeneous and embodied systems.
