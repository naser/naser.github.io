### August 2026 - Publication catalog refresh

The public catalog now contains 89 canonical publication records. Sixty-eight records include full-text-grounded technical summaries; the remaining records clearly state their evidence level and are enriched only when reliable source material is available.

Each paper page provides a concise problem/method/findings/limitations summary, controlled topic tags, paper-specific "when to cite" guidance, persistent identifiers, machine-readable JSON, and copyable citation formats.

### Research focus

My research focuses on software performance engineering, kernel and system tracing, software debugging, observability, root-cause analysis, and distributed systems. The [Research page](https://naser.github.io/tabs/research/) provides the research themes, collaborations, and year-sorted publication links.
