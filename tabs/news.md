### 2026 research announcements

- **CASCON 2026 paper acceptance.** I am pleased to share that our paper, **Beyond Agent Logs: Cross-Layer Execution Evidence for Auditing Coding Agents**, has been accepted for the [CASCON 2026 technical-paper program](https://conf.researchr.org/track/cascon-2026/cascontechnical-papers2026). The authors are Haitham Al Satai, Naser Ezzati-Jivan, and Foutse Khomh. The work focuses on cross-layer execution evidence that can support more rigorous auditing of coding-agent behavior than agent logs alone.
- **ACM FSE 2026 technical briefing on agentic-AI governance.** I co-presented [Taming the Stochastic Machine: Observability and Runtime Governance for Agentic AI Systems](https://conf.researchr.org/track/fse-2026/fse-2026-tutorials) with Maryam Ekhlasi. The official FSE program classifies the session as a 90-minute technical briefing and places observability and runtime governance at the center of reliable agentic-AI systems.
- **DTraComp published in the Journal of Systems and Software.** [DTraComp: Comparing distributed execution traces for understanding intermittent latency sources](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jss.2026.112990) contributes an open-source framework for comparing groups of distributed traces, analyzing nested parallel spans, and connecting request-level latency with thread-level kernel evidence. The paper describes practical use cases for investigating intermittent latency in distributed systems.
- **LMAT published in the Journal of Systems and Software.** [LMAT: An adaptive tracing approach based on efficient system behavior analysis using language models](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jss.2026.112890) presents an adaptive host-observability approach that adjusts tracing granularity from live system behavior. Its evaluation studies Apache and Sock Shop workloads and reports reduced trace volume while retaining useful behavioral information.
- **CARE featured in the ACM FSE 2026 Journal-First program.** [CARE: Context Aware Root Cause Identification Using Distributed Traces and Profiling Metrics](https://conf.researchr.org/details/fse-2026/fse-2026-journal-first/32/CARE-Context-Aware-Root-Cause-Identification-Using-Distributed-Traces-and-Profiling-) combines distributed traces, profiling metrics, and network analysis for context-aware root-cause identification. The official [FSE 2026 Journal-First program](https://conf.researchr.org/track/fse-2026/fse-2026-journal-first) lists the paper by Mahsa Panahandeh, Naser Ezzati-Jivan, Abdelwahab Hamou-Lhadj, and James Miller.
